Log in to Hive Assessments

Getting into your account for the first time, and turning on two-factor sign-in while you’re there.

4 steps2 min readUpdated 3 days ago
01

Open the sign-in page

Go to hive.app/login on your phone or laptop. If your team uses a custom address, it’s the link in your invitation email.

You’ll know you’re in the right place when you see the Hive logo above a Welcome to Hive heading.

02

Enter your credentials

Tap the Email field and type the address your invitation was sent to. Then tap Password and enter the password you set.

If you haven’t set one yet, use Forgot password? to have a link sent to you. Then press Log in.

03

Land on your dashboard

Hive opens on Assessments. Anything your team has already set up is listed here; a brand-new account shows an empty list and a Create assessment button.

04

Turn on two-factor sign-in

Open Settings → Security and switch on Two-factor authentication. Hive shows a QR code — scan it with your authenticator app and enter the six-digit code to confirm.

Did this answer your question?

Your answer goes to the Hive team. It’s anonymous.

These four are separated, and none of them apologise.
