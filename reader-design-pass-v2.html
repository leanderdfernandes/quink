01 — Home

One band, not two. The logo and name live inside the header surface at 46px and 25px, so the customer's identity is the first thing on the page instead of chrome above it. Four header treatments, all derived from the one brand colour — Solid is the default because it can't turn grey. The old 7% tint produced flat grey for any desaturated brand; Tint is now 13% and is the option, not the default. Category headings sit in a sticky left rail so rows stop stretching to 1600px.

02 — Article

The band goes compact — masthead and search only, so the customer's brand is still present on every view without repeating the hero. The serif is gone: it read as a font change rather than a voice, and it's the exact pairing that reads as machine-made. Editorial now comes from scale, measure, mono metadata and hairlines. The spine is the signature — spend the boldness there and nowhere else.

Log in to Memory

Getting into your account for the first time, and turning on two-factor sign-in while you're there.

4 steps2 min readUpdated 3 days ago
01

Open the sign-in page

Go to memory.app/login on your phone or laptop. If your team uses a custom address, it's the link in your invitation email.

You'll know you're in the right place when you see the logo above a Welcome back heading.

02

Enter your credentials

Tap the Email field and type the address your invitation was sent to. Then tap Password and enter the password you set.

If you haven't set one yet, use Forgot password? to have a link sent to you. Then press Log in.

03

Land on your library

Memory opens on All collections. Anything your team has already saved is listed here; a brand-new account shows an empty list and a New collection button.

04

Turn on two-factor sign-in

Open Settings → Security and switch on Two-factor authentication. Memory shows a QR code — scan it with your authenticator app and enter the six-digit code to confirm.
