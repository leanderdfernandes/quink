## Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings, or by emailing
support@quink.online. There is no cancellation fee, no notice period, and no phone call.

When you cancel:

- Your subscription stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for.
- At the end of that period, billing stops and the payment mandate is cancelled.
- Your account moves to the free plan. Your articles remain, subject to the free plan's
  30-day limit described in the Terms.
- Any custom domain is released and stops resolving to your help center.

Cancelling does **not** delete your account or your content. That is a separate action in
Settings, and it is permanent.

## Refunds

**First subscription payment — 7 days.** If Quink isn't right for you, email
support@quink.online within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. You
don't need to give a reason.

**Renewals.** We don't refund renewal payments. Cancel before the renewal date and you won't
be charged. If a renewal caught you by surprise and you haven't used the service in that
period, email us anyway — we'd rather sort it out than argue.

**Annual plans.** Cancel any time; the plan runs to the end of the paid year. We don't refund
the unused portion, except in the 7-day window above.

**If we're at fault** — a sustained outage, a charge that shouldn't have happened, a
duplicate payment — we refund it. Email us and we'll fix it without a process.

**What isn't refundable.** Generations already processed. Each one costs us real money at the
moment it runs, so we can't return an allowance that's already been spent.

## How refunds are processed

Approved refunds go back through Razorpay to the original payment method. We initiate within
2 business days of approving. Your bank or card issuer typically takes a further 5–7 business
days to show it.

We do not issue refunds by any other method, and we will never ask you for card details,
UPI PIN, OTP or bank credentials to process one. Anyone who does is not us.

## Chargebacks

If something's wrong, email us first. We answer support@quink.online personally and usually
within a day. A chargeback costs us a fee and gets you your money slower than we would.
